About Dhananjay Narula

Dhananjay Narula

Lead Developer & Technical Architect

Dhananjay Narula is the developer and technical architect behind AnkJyōtish, the numerology platform that combines ancient wisdom with modern technology. As a software engineer, Dhananjay designed, developed, and deployed the AnkJyōtish website and mobile experience, bringing the vision of accessible numerology to life.

With a passion for creating seamless user experiences and robust technical solutions, Dhananjay has implemented numerological algorithms, interactive visualizations, and intuitive interfaces that make ancient numerology principles accessible to a wider audience. His expertise spans frontend technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with practical product design and implementation.

Dhananjay's commitment to excellence is evident in every aspect of AnkJyōtish, from the responsive design that works across devices to the calculations that provide accurate and meaningful insights. He has integrated features such as Vedic numerology grids, compatibility analysis, name numerology, and personalized destiny reports while maintaining a strong focus on usability.

As the technical foundation of AnkJyōtish, Dhananjay continues to improve the platform so users receive accurate and accessible numerological guidance. His work bridges the gap between traditional numerology and digital accessibility.

For technical inquiries, collaborations, or custom development projects, Dhananjay welcomes contact from fellow developers, numerologists, and organizations interested in technology for spiritual and personal growth.
